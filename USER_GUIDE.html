Reco User Guide

Double-entry accounting for your business — unified sales invoicing, payments, receipts, ledgers, and reports. Web and mobile share the same rules.

Web Admin Mobile API Tally-style books Multi-company

Welcome

Reco keeps books clean: every transaction is a balanced voucher (Debit = Credit) and posts to ledgers automatically.

Masters

Ledgers, parties, goods items, tax rates, and financial years.

Day-to-day work

One Sales Invoice for goods and services, purchase invoices, payments, receipts, adjustments.

Reports

Day Book, Cash/Bank Book, Ledger, Trial Balance, P&L, Balance Sheet, outstanding.

Mobile

Same accounting behaviour as web via the Reco API.

Current product shape There is a single menu Sales Invoice (items + services on one bill). There is no separate “Service Sale Invoices” menu. Cash / Bank / OD are ledgers with a transaction mode — there is no separate Bank Accounts module. Use Cash Book / Bank Book instead of a Cash Flow report.

Quick Start

  1. Register or log in
    Create your company (or join an existing one), then open the admin dashboard at /admin/login.
  2. Set the Financial Year
    Go to Settings → Financial Years. Create the year and mark it as Current.
  3. Check default ledgers
    Open Masters → Ledgers. Cash / Bank / Sales / Purchase / Tax ledgers are created for you. Add opening balances if needed.
  4. Add parties
    Create customers (debtors) and suppliers (creditors).
  5. Add goods and service items
    Both live under Masters → Items. Choose Goods or Service when creating. Services are non-stockable items that post via their income account (default Sales Revenue).
  6. Create a Sales Invoice
    Transactions → Sales Invoice → add item and/or service lines → save (posts income) → Record Payment when money arrives.
  7. Review books
    Open Day Book / Ledger / Trial Balance to confirm Debit = Credit.

Login & Registration

Register a new company

Open the public Register page, fill company and admin details, then submit. After approval (if required), log in at /admin/login.

Login

  • Email + password
  • Use the sidebar: Dashboard, Masters, Transactions (Vouchers), Reports, Settings
Tip Keep one current financial year open. Creating or editing vouchers/invoices is blocked if the year is closed or the date is outside the FY (period lock).

Dashboard

After login you see receivables, payables, recent activity, and charts. Use it as a daily overview — details live in Reports and Transactions.

List screens (invoices, vouchers, masters) show newest first by default.

Ledgers (Chart of Accounts)

Menu: Masters → Ledgers

Every money movement posts to a ledger. Types: Asset, Liability, Income, Expense, Equity.

Cash, Bank & OD

When creating an asset ledger, set Transaction mode:

ModeUse for
CashPetty cash / till — Cash Book and Paid From / Received In
BankBank accounts — Bank Book and settlements
ODOverdraft — can pay even with low/negative available balance

Sales Revenue for goods and services

Goods and service items default to the system Sales Revenue income ledger. You can override an item’s income account if needed. Income ledgers are still used for general income vouchers — not as the sales line catalog for services.

Opening balance

Enter opening balance + Debit/Credit when creating a ledger. Reco posts a balanced opening entry so Trial Balance stays clean.

No separate Bank Accounts menu Manage all cash and bank ledgers here. Payments and receipts pick them from “Paid From” / “Received In”.

Parties (Customers & Suppliers)

Menu: Masters → Parties

  • Debtor — customer (receivable)
  • Creditor — supplier (payable)

Each party is linked to its own ledger. Opening balances on parties also post through the books.

Open a party’s details (name or eye icon) to see ledger / transaction history, with Excel / PDF export and pagination.

On invoice and voucher screens you can use Quick Add Party without leaving the form.

Items & Categories

Menu: Masters → Items and Item Categories

Goods items

Stockable products used on Sales and Purchase invoice lines (code, name, rate, tax, unit). Categories help organize the list.

Service items

Create services from the same Masters → Items screen (choose Service). They are non-stockable items (type=service), billable by quantity (e.g. hours), and posted to accounts via income_account_id (default Sales Revenue). On Sales Invoice, pick them from the Services group in the line dropdown.

Item history

Open an item’s details to see sales / purchase history, export Excel or PDF, and browse pages of transactions.

Books-first Reco does not run full perpetual inventory or COGS journals as a separate costing engine. Goods stock fields support invoice and history views; accounting focuses on sales, purchase, and cash books.

Tax Rates

Menu: Masters → Tax Rates

Define GST / tax percentages used on invoice lines. Inactive rates are hidden from new invoices.

Financial Years

Menu: Settings → Financial Years

  • Create a year (e.g. 1 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2026)
  • Set as Current — new vouchers and invoices use this year. Closing balances are carried forward as opening entries.
  • Close — locks the year; you cannot post or edit dated transactions in a closed year
Period lock Create / edit / cancel is blocked when the financial year is closed, or when the voucher/invoice date falls outside the current FY range.

Sales Invoice

Menu: Transactions → Sales Invoice (single menu — items and services together)

Unified billing One invoice can include goods item lines, service item lines, or both. There is no separate Service Sale Invoices screen.
  1. Choose customer (debtor), invoice date, and due date.
  2. Add line rows from one dropdown:
    • Goods — goods items (qty, unit price, discount, tax)
    • Services — service items (editable qty for hours/units, unit price, discount, tax) — same item_id line payload
    Description fills from the item description and stays read-only on the form.
  3. Save — Reco creates the invoice and posts an income voucher (Dr Party, Cr Sales Revenue / item income account / tax as mapped).
  4. Open the invoice → Record Payment when money is received (mode + cash/bank + amount + date). A Receipt voucher is posted and linked to the invoice.

Invoice numbers continue company-wide (e.g. INV-000002), even after a new financial year starts.

Old URLs under Service Sale Invoices redirect to this Sales Invoice module.

Purchase Invoices

Menu: Transactions → Purchase Invoices

  1. Choose supplier (creditor), dates, optional supplier bill number / terms.
  2. Add goods item lines only (qty, rate, tax, discount). Purchase does not use service/income lines.
  3. Save — posts an expense voucher.
  4. Record Payment posts a Payment voucher against the bill.
Tip For buying a service from a supplier, use a Payment voucher (or expense ledger) — not a purchase “service line”.

Payments

Menu: Transactions → Payments

Use this when you pay money out (supplier, expense, etc.) — Tally style:

1. Mode (Cash / Bank / OD) → 2. Paid From → 3. Particulars → 4. Amount
  • Particulars can be a party or a ledger account
  • Reco builds the voucher: Dr Particulars, Cr Cash/Bank
  • Insufficient cash/bank balance is blocked (OD is exempt)
  • Posted immediately to ledgers

Receipts

Menu: Transactions → Receipts

Use this when you receive money — same form pattern as Payments:

1. Mode → 2. Received In → 3. Particulars → 4. Amount
  • Posting: Dr Cash/Bank, Cr Particulars
  • Sales Invoice “Record Payment” creates these receipts for you automatically

Adjustments (Journal)

Menu: Transactions → Adjustments

For corrections and transfers that are not a simple payment/receipt:

  • Add at least two lines
  • Each line: Particulars + Debit or Credit + Amount
  • Total Debit must equal Total Credit
Tip Prefer Payments / Receipts for money movement. Use Adjustments for ledger-to-ledger journals only. Do not create income/expense vouchers by hand — use Sales Invoice / Purchase Invoice.

All Vouchers

Menu: Transactions → All Vouchers

Browse every posted voucher (payments, receipts, adjustments, and invoice postings). Open a voucher to see header details and lines side by side. Newest vouchers appear first.

Reports

Menu: Analytics → Reports (or Reports section in your sidebar)

All reports support filters (date / financial year where applicable). Clickable names open the related voucher, ledger, invoice, or party detail.

ReportWhat it shows
Day BookAll posted voucher lines for a selected date (optional FY filter)
Cash BookCash ledger receipts & payments with opening / closing
Bank BookBank / OD ledger statement for the period
LedgerOne account’s running balance (voucher-wise)
Trial BalanceAll ledger Debit / Credit totals — must balance
Profit & LossIncome vs expenses for the FY
Balance SheetAssets vs liabilities + equity
ReceivablesDebtors with outstanding debit balances
PayablesCreditors with outstanding credit balances
Drill-down Voucher numbers open the voucher; account names open the ledger; party names open party details where available; invoice links open Sales Invoice / Purchase Invoice.

Export PDF / Excel

  • Most report screens: PDF and Excel next to Filter
  • Sales Invoice detail: PDF from the invoice page
  • Item / Party history: Excel and PDF from the detail screens
  • List tables: Excel / PDF buttons on DataTables where enabled

Company Settings & Theme

Menu: Settings

  • Company — profile, address, GST / PAN, currency, timezone
  • Theme — primary / secondary / sidebar / header colours and dark mode; live preview while you change colours; Save to apply for the company
  • Accounting — map tax ledgers (sales tax, purchase tax, TDS, TCS, cess) where used
  • Financial Years — see above

Subscription & Billing

If your plan is managed in Reco, open subscription from Settings / billing screens to view plan, invoices, and renewals (Razorpay where enabled).

Support, Notifications & Audit

  • Notifications — bell icon in the header for system alerts
  • Support tickets — raise and track help requests with the platform team
  • Audit logs — who changed what (users with permission)

Mobile App / API

The mobile API mirrors web accounting. Prefer these endpoints:

AreaUse
SalesGET/POST /api/v1/sales-invoices — list all by default; create with lines and/or service_lines
Line pickerGET /api/v1/items/dropdown returns { items, services } (goods items + service-type items)
Items masterGET /api/v1/items — paginated items list (filter by type goods/service)
PurchaseItem (goods) lines only; supports payment/delivery terms
Payments / ReceiptsSame Tally fields: payment_mode, cash/bank account, particulars rows
ThemeGET/PUT /api/v1/settings/theme
PDFExport responses include content_base64 for offline-friendly download
Sales Invoice Use unified /api/v1/sales-invoices with lines and/or service_lines. Invoice numbers follow INV-202627/0001.

Swagger: /api/documentation

Tips & FAQ

Where did Service Sale Invoices go?

They are merged into Sales Invoice. Add service lines from the Services group on the same form. Old bookmarks redirect automatically.

How do I bill a service?

Go to Masters → Items → Add, choose Service, and save. On Sales Invoice, pick it under the Services group. It posts via the item’s income account (default Sales Revenue).

Why can’t I save a voucher?

  • Debit must equal Credit
  • Date must fall inside the current open financial year
  • For Payments: cash/bank account must match the selected mode, and balance must be enough (unless OD)

Invoice number already exists?

Numbers are unique across the company. After a new FY, numbering continues (INV-000002…); it does not restart at 000001.

Where did Bank Accounts go?

They are ledgers now. Create an Asset ledger with transaction mode Bank (or Cash / OD).

Where is Cash Flow?

Use Cash Book and Bank Book reports.

Can purchase invoices include services?

No — purchase lines are goods items only. Pay service suppliers via Payment vouchers.

Recommended daily flow

Sales Invoice → Record Payment → Day Book → Trial Balance
Need help? Open a support ticket from the admin panel, or ask your company admin to check Audit Logs and Financial Year status.
