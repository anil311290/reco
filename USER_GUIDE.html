Reco User Guide

Simple double-entry accounting for your business — invoices, payments, receipts, ledgers, and reports.

Web Admin Mobile API Tally-style books Multi-company

Welcome

Reco helps you keep books clean: every transaction is a balanced voucher (Debit = Credit), posted to ledgers automatically.

Masters

Ledgers, parties, items, tax rates, and financial years.

Day-to-day work

Sales / purchase / service invoices, payments, receipts, adjustments.

Reports

Day Book, Cash/Bank Book, Ledger, Trial Balance, P&L, Balance Sheet, outstanding.

Mobile

Same accounting rules as web via the Reco API.

Removed / not used There is no separate Bank Accounts module. Cash, Bank, and OD are normal ledgers with a transaction mode. There is no Cash Flow report — use Cash Book / Bank Book instead. Stock / inventory costing (COGS) is not part of the accounting flow; items are for invoice lines only.

Quick Start

  1. Register or log in
    Create your company (or join an existing one), then open the admin dashboard.
  2. Set the Financial Year
    Go to Settings → Financial Years. Create the year and mark it as Current.
  3. Check default ledgers
    Open Masters → Ledgers. Cash / Bank / Sales / Purchase / Tax ledgers are created for you. Add opening balances if needed.
  4. Add parties
    Create customers (debtors) and suppliers (creditors). Each party gets a linked ledger.
  5. Start billing
    Create a Sales or Service invoice → it posts income automatically → record payment when money comes in.
  6. Review books
    Open Day Book / Ledger / Trial Balance to confirm Debit = Credit.

Login & Registration

Register a new company

Open the public Register page, fill company and admin details, then submit. After approval (if required), log in at /admin/login.

Login

  • Email + password
  • Use the sidebar to move between Dashboard, Masters, Transactions, Reports, and Settings
Tip Keep one current financial year open. Creating or editing vouchers/invoices is blocked if the year is closed or the date is outside the FY (period lock).

Dashboard

After login you see a snapshot of your books: receivables, payables, recent activity, and charts. Use it as a daily overview — details live in Reports and Transactions.

Ledgers (Chart of Accounts)

Menu: Masters → Ledgers

Every money movement posts to a ledger. Types: Asset, Liability, Income, Expense, Equity.

Cash, Bank & OD

When creating an asset ledger, set Transaction mode:

ModeUse for
CashPetty cash / till — used in Cash Book and as Paid From / Received In
BankBank accounts — used in Bank Book and settlements
ODOverdraft — can pay even with low/negative available balance

Opening balance

Enter opening balance + Debit/Credit type when creating a ledger. Reco posts a balanced opening entry (via suspense) so Trial Balance stays clean.

No separate Bank Accounts menu Manage all cash and bank ledgers here. Payments and receipts pick them from “Paid From” / “Received In”.

Parties (Customers & Suppliers)

Menu: Masters → Parties

  • Debtor — customer (receivable)
  • Creditor — supplier (payable)

Each party is linked to its own ledger. Opening balances on parties also post through the books.

On invoice and voucher screens you can use Quick Add Party without leaving the form.

Items & Categories

Menu: Masters → Items and Item Categories

Items are used on Sales and Purchase invoice lines (name, rate, tax). Categories help organize the item list.

Books-only Reco does not run perpetual inventory or COGS journals. Stock quantity fields (if shown) are for reference — accounting focuses on sales/purchase and cash books.

Tax Rates

Menu: Masters → Tax Rates

Define GST / tax percentages used on invoice lines. Inactive rates are hidden from new invoices.

Financial Years

Menu: Settings → Financial Years

  • Create a year (e.g. 1 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2026)
  • Set as Current — new vouchers and invoices use this year. Closing balances are carried forward into the new year as opening entries.
  • Close — locks the year; you cannot post or edit dated transactions in a closed year
Period lock Create / edit / cancel is blocked when the financial year is closed, or when the voucher/invoice date falls outside the current FY range.

Sales Invoices (Item)

Menu: Transactions → Sales Invoices

  1. Choose customer (debtor), invoice date, due date.
  2. Add item lines (qty, rate, tax, discount).
  3. Save — Reco creates the invoice and posts an income voucher (Dr Party, Cr Sales / Tax).
  4. Open the invoice → Record Payment when money is received (mode + cash/bank + amount + date). A Receipt voucher is posted and linked to the invoice.

Invoice numbers continue company-wide (e.g. INV-000002), even after a new financial year starts.

Service Sales Invoices

Menu: Transactions → Service Sale Invoices

Same flow as sales invoices, but lines are services (description + amount + tax) instead of stock items. Record Payment works the same way (Receipt voucher).

Purchase Invoices

Menu: Transactions → Purchase Invoices

  1. Choose supplier (creditor), dates, optional supplier reference.
  2. Add item or service lines.
  3. Save — posts an expense voucher.
  4. Record Payment posts a Payment voucher against the bill.

Payments

Menu: Transactions → Payments

Use this when you pay money out (supplier, expense, etc.) — Tally style:

1. Mode (Cash / Bank / OD) → 2. Paid From → 3. Particulars (creditors) → 4. Amount
  • Reco builds the voucher: Dr Particulars, Cr Cash/Bank
  • Insufficient cash/bank balance is blocked (OD is exempt)
  • Posted immediately to ledgers and journals

Receipts

Menu: Transactions → Receipts

Use this when you receive money — same form as Payments:

1. Mode → 2. Received In → 3. Particulars (debtors) → 4. Amount
  • Posting: Dr Cash/Bank, Cr Particulars
  • Invoice “Record Payment” creates these receipts for you automatically

Adjustments (Journal)

Menu: Transactions → Adjustments

For corrections and transfers that are not a simple payment/receipt:

  • Add at least two lines
  • Each line: Particulars + Debit or Credit + Amount
  • Total Debit must equal Total Credit
Tip Prefer Payments / Receipts for money movement. Use Adjustments for ledger-to-ledger journals only.

Reports

Menu: Analytics → Reports

All reports support filters (date / financial year where applicable). Clickable names open the related voucher, ledger, or party detail.

ReportWhat it shows
Day BookAll posted voucher lines for a selected date (optional FY filter)
Cash BookCash ledger receipts & payments with opening / closing
Bank BookBank / OD ledger statement for the period
LedgerOne account’s running balance (voucher-wise)
Trial BalanceAll ledger Debit / Credit totals — must balance
Profit & LossIncome vs expenses for the FY
Balance SheetAssets vs liabilities + equity
ReceivablesDebtors with outstanding debit balances
PayablesCreditors with outstanding credit balances
Drill-down In reports, voucher numbers open the voucher; account names open the ledger; party names open party details where available.

Export PDF / Excel

On most report screens use the PDF and Excel buttons next to Filter. Day Book PDF also respects the selected financial year.

Sales invoices have their own PDF from the invoice detail page.

Company Settings & Theme

Menu: Settings

  • Company profile and preferences
  • Theme colours / dark mode
  • Financial Years (see above)

Subscription & Billing

If your plan is managed in Reco, open subscription from Settings / billing screens to view plan, invoices, and renewals (Razorpay where enabled).

Support, Notifications & Audit

  • Notifications — bell icon in the header for system alerts
  • Support tickets — raise and track help requests
  • Audit logs — who changed what (for users with permission)

Mobile App

The mobile API mirrors web accounting:

  • Payments / Receipts use the same Tally fields (payment_mode, cash/bank account, particulars rows)
  • Adjustments use journal rows (Debit / Credit)
  • Invoice settlement needs amount + mode + cash/bank account
  • Helpers: cash-bank list and particulars lists for form dropdowns
  • Offline sync endpoints keep devices up to date

API docs (Swagger): /api/documentation

Tips & FAQ

Why can’t I save a voucher?

  • Debit must equal Credit
  • Date must fall inside the current open financial year
  • For Payments: cash/bank account must match the selected mode, and balance must be enough (unless OD)

Invoice number already exists?

Numbers are unique across the company. After a new FY, numbering continues (INV-000002…), it does not restart at 000001.

Where did Bank Accounts go?

They are ledgers now. Create an Asset ledger with transaction mode Bank (or Cash / OD).

Where is Cash Flow?

Use Cash Book and Bank Book reports.

Recommended daily flow

Invoices → Record Payment → Day Book → Trial Balance
Need help? Open a support ticket from the admin panel, or ask your company admin to check Audit Logs and Financial Year status.
